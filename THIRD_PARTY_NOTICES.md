### Avatars and VRM models

No VRM avatar is redistributed in this repository. The Unity recording in
`examples/human-capture/sample_output/03_unity_vrm/unity_vrm_animation.mp4`
shows a third-party avatar model rendered locally. **Avatar models have their own
licenses and usage conditions, frequently restricting commercial use, modification
and redistribution.** Check the license of any avatar you use, and do not assume
one shown here is freely reusable.

To reproduce the Unity output you supply your own VRM 1.0 avatar — for example the
VRM Consortium's freely available sample avatars, under their own terms.

### Robot descriptions — `adapters/isaac_sim_groot_g1/g1_kinematics.json`

That file is **derived from a third-party robot description** and is not
Apache-2.0 material of this repository. It holds joint origins, axes, limits and
link masses read out of `g1_29dof_with_hand.urdf`, the official **Unitree G1**
description published by [Unitree Robotics](https://github.com/unitreerobotics)
(the `unitree_ros` G1 description is BSD-3-Clause); the copy those numbers were
read from ships inside NVIDIA's GR00T-WholeBodyControl. **Verify the terms that
apply to your use before redistributing it.**

What it deliberately does **not** contain: no visual or collision meshes, no
inertia tensors, no vendor artwork. The shells in the v1 stage's
`g1_robot.usda` are capsules generated from those joint origins by this
repository's own code, so no Unitree mesh is redistributed here and that render
is not the vendor's model.

`07_groot_g1_v2/` shows the vendor's model instead, and does it **by
reference**: `robot_v2.usda` holds one `references = @…/g1_29dof_with_hand_rev_1_0.usd@`
line pointing at NVIDIA's asset server, and `--asset-root` redirects that at a
local mirror. The same applies to the props the object catalog names. Nothing
under `07_groot_g1_v2/` contains vendor geometry: opening the stage fetches it,
and the repository stays free of it. `object_catalog.json` records asset paths
for the same reason — they are addresses, not content.

The 43-DoF state/action ordering and the `modality.json` grouping the adapter
writes follow the **Isaac Lab Arena** G1 embodiment
([IsaacLab-Arena](https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab-Arena), Apache-2.0) and
the [LeRobot](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot) v2.1 dataset layout
(Apache-2.0). Those are interface descriptions, reimplemented here; no Arena or
LeRobot code is vendored. No **GR00T** model weights are used, downloaded or
redistributed by this repository, and no GR00T policy is run by any adapter in
it — v1's `adapter_report.json` records `"policy_was_run": false`.
`07_groot_g1_v2/` runs PhysX rather than a policy: its joint angles are what a
position-drive controller tracked, and `settle_final.json` records the residual
per joint.

### Object detection vocabulary

Object labels come from an EfficientDet-Lite model trained on the **COCO** 80-class
vocabulary. COCO's annotations and terms are those of the
[COCO dataset](https://cocodataset.org/).
