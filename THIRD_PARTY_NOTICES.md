### Sample outputs — `examples/*/sample_output/`

These are the recorded results of running the demos, kept so the repository is
useful without executing anything. They include:

| Content | Note |
|---|---|
| `source/source_video.mp4` | The source clip used for the demo. **Verify your own rights before reusing or redistributing this footage.** |
| `01_mediapipe_overlay/`, `05_comparison/` | Derived from the source video; the same rights considerations apply. |
| `04_behavior_dataset/timeline/frames/*.jpg` | Frames extracted from the source video. |
| `03_unity_vrm/unity_vrm_animation.mp4` | A Unity recording using a third-party VRM avatar model (see below). |
| `04_behavior_dataset/captions/` | Text generated by the Gemini API; subject to Google's terms. |
| `02_mujoco/`, `03_unity_vrm/motion.vrma`, `.../sample_output/` derived data | Behavior data derived from the above. |

**Personal data.** The source video depicts a person. If you reuse this material,
consider the privacy and likeness rights that apply in your jurisdiction. If you
capture your own data, the same applies to your subjects — behavior data is
biometric-adjacent by nature.

### Avatars and VRM models

No VRM avatar is redistributed in this repository. The Unity recording in
`examples/human-capture/sample_output/03_unity_vrm/unity_vrm_animation.mp4`
shows a third-party avatar model rendered locally. **Avatar models have their own
licenses and usage conditions, frequently restricting commercial use, modification
and redistribution.** Check the license of any avatar you use, and do not assume
one shown here is freely reusable.

To reproduce the Unity output you supply your own VRM 1.0 avatar — for example the
VRM Consortium's freely available sample avatars, under their own terms.

### Object detection vocabulary

Object labels come from an EfficientDet-Lite model trained on the **COCO** 80-class
vocabulary. COCO's annotations and terms are those of the
[COCO dataset](https://cocodataset.org/).

## API keys and credentials

No credentials of any kind are stored in this repository. The optional Gemini
features read `GEMINI_API_KEY` from Colab Secrets or the environment at runtime,
and the value is never written to disk or into notebook output.
